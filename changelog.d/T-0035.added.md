- `housekeep.py worktrees` and `housekeep.py all` now also report worktrees and local branches of
  tasks that are `done` or `abandoned`, whether merged or not, and branches that have no worktree.
  The command only reports and removes nothing. It exits 1 on any finding, as before, so a leftover
  of a finished task now makes it exit 1 where it used to exit 0.
- The report verify line accepts `passed (commit <hash>; gate <record-key>; <counts>)`, which names
  the gate record the verifier wrote. `report.py validate` still accepts the older form without the
  gate part, and the report template documents the new form.
