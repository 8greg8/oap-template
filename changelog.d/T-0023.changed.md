- Breaking: a push or a `scripts/gitflow.sh finish` that carries a `feat`, `fix` or breaking commit
  now needs a changelog fragment in `changelog.d/`, and is rejected without one. The pre-push hook
  checks each pushed branch from its merge base with `origin/develop` (`origin/main` for
  `hotfix/*`), and `main` and `develop` from the remote commit to the pushed one. Tags, deleted
  refs and `.agents/`-only pushes are not checked.
