# Report — T-0035: Report leftover worktrees and record the gate in the verify line

## Summary

`housekeep.py worktrees` also reports worktrees and local branches of `done` or `abandoned`
tasks, merged or not, including branches with no worktree. `report.py validate` accepts the verify
line `passed (commit <hash>; gate <key>; <counts>)`, rejects a malformed `gate` part and does not
require one. The template documents it. After the orchestrator's amendment of the task on
`develop` (`9c90815`), the branch was conformed: key length 12 to 64, a breaking commit and footer,
a fragment that follows the pre-release rules. All acceptance criteria are met.

  (`6879908`) conforms the branch without rewriting pushed history: key pattern 7 to 64 changed to

  - a secondary worktree on the branch of a `done` or `abandoned` task, merged or not;

## Known limitations

- The validator takes 12 to 64 lowercase hex characters, as the amended task says. It does not
  check that the key names a real record or that its length is 40 or 64; the full key is 40 or 64
  characters per T-0033, and the verify line may cite a prefix.
- The gate part is optional, so a new report can omit it and still validate. The plan only asks
  that `validate` accept the form and keep the old one valid.
- A finished task's branch checked out in the primary checkout is reported as a `BRANCH` finding.
- The check reads local branches only. Remote branches are not reported.

## Lessons learned

- A scratch-repo test for `housekeep.py` must patch `load_tasks`; otherwise the real repository's
  task files decide the result, and a branch named `feat/T-0001-...` matches a real finished task.
- The task-ID in a task branch name, not the full branch name, is the stable key to a task file:
  the type prefix and slug can differ from what `Task.branch` generates.
